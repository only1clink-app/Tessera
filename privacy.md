Tessera Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 23, 2026
Introduction
Tessera helps families preserve and share the stories behind their heirlooms and treasured possessions. Protecting your privacy—and your family's privacy—is central to how Tessera is designed.
This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored by the app and how that information is handled.
Information Stored by Tessera
Tessera may store the following information that you choose to add:
	•	Photos of heirlooms and family items
	•	Video recordings
	•	Audio recordings of stories and memories
	•	Item names and titles
	•	Notes and descriptions
	•	Transcripts generated from recordings
	•	Optional location or place names associated with an item
Where Your Information Is Stored
Your content is stored on your device.
If you enable family sharing features, your content is synchronized through your personal Apple iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit services. Tessera does not operate its own servers and does not receive, access, view, store, or process your content on external servers.
Storage and synchronization through iCloud are subject to Apple's privacy practices and terms.
Information We Collect
Tessera does not collect personal information.
Specifically:
	•	No analytics are collected
	•	No advertising identifiers are collected
	•	No tracking technologies are used
	•	No usage data is transmitted to the developer
	•	No personal information is sold or shared
Because Tessera does not operate backend servers, we do not have access to your stories, photos, recordings, transcripts, or other content.
Sharing
Your stories and heirloom records remain private.
Content is visible only to family members or individuals that you explicitly choose to share with through the app's sharing features. You may remove a person's access at any time.
Permissions Used by the App
Tessera may request the following permissions:
Camera and Photos
Used to capture and attach photos of heirlooms and family items.
Microphone
Used to record stories, memories, and audio descriptions.
Speech Recognition
Used to transcribe audio recordings into text on your device.
These permissions are used solely to provide app functionality and are not used for advertising, tracking, or analytics.
Data Deletion
You may delete any item, photo, recording, transcript, or other content within the app at any time.
If you remove the app from your device, locally stored content will be removed. If you use iCloud synchronization, you may also delete associated data from your iCloud account.
Children's Privacy
Tessera is intended for adults preserving family memories and heirloom histories. The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and Tessera does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
